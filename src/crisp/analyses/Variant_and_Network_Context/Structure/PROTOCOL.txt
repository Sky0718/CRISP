Relative connectivity and allocation correspondence

This analysis relates changes in a candidate's connectivity rank within its
model-specific query to gains and losses of selected allocation. It retains
all categories and both directions in a finite, post-exposure descriptive
comparison.

Inputs and population
The inputs are the G0_T700 and G1_T700 common-domain binary graphs from
Allocation_and_Graph_Controls/Graph_Controls: gene_degrees.csv,
ALLOCATION_FREEZE.json, graph_metadata.csv, both complete edge files and
state-level endpoints for reconciliation.

The domain contains 7,399 genes and 85 models from 83 donors. Queries,
categories and allocations are fixed inputs. Structural endpoints require
no functional labels. Model weight is

alpha_s = 1/(83 * number_of_models_for_donor_s).

Opportunity and allocation calculations use exact rational arithmetic.

Categories
ALL is accompanied by two separate exhaustive partitions:
Core: BOTH_CORE, ORGANOID_ONLY, CELL_LINE_ONLY, NOT_LISTED_IN_EITHER.
Role: ONCOGENE, TSG, DUAL_ONCOGENE_TSG, OTHER_OR_UNSPECIFIED, UNLISTED.
The partitions overlap each other, so each reconciles separately with ALL.

Domain structure
Each category contributes gene count, old/new arithmetic mean and median
degree, isolate counts and mean whole-domain degree midrank percentile.
For n>1, L strictly lower degrees and T equal degrees define percentile

[L+(T-1)/2]/(n-1).

This degree-increasing rank retains tied isolates and distinguishes
absolute connectivity contraction from relative position.

Query opportunity
Category opportunity is sum_s alpha_s * eligible_gene_count_s. The
uniform-query ten-slot reference is

10 * sum_s alpha_s * (category_query_count_s/full_query_count_s).

Both are constant across graph states because queries are unchanged.
Further endpoints are old/new donor-weighted connected eligible counts
(degree>0) and pooled category-eligible within-query midrank percentile,

(sum_s alpha_s sum_g percentile_sg)/(sum_s alpha_s n_category,s).

This pooled mean weights every eligible model-gene opportunity by alpha_s.
Empty category denominators yield null; within-query rank requires n>1.

Allocation correspondence
The four contrasts are:
DawnRank joint: G0_T700_H0 -> G1_T700_H1.
DawnRank graph at H0: G0_T700_H0 -> G1_T700_H0.
DawnRank graph at H1: G0_T700_H1 -> G1_T700_H1.
Same-query degree: G0_T700_DEGREE -> G1_T700_DEGREE.

Each eligible model-gene identity is assigned its exact within-query degree
percentile change: negative, zero or positive. In each category, contrast
and stratum, positive and negative allocation differences are separated
before donor averaging to give gross gain, gross loss and net change.
Complete model-gene and donor records accompany these summaries; the
model-gene records are nested observations rather than independent
biological replicates.

Numerical checks
The graph sizes are 119,423 old edges and 65,783 new edges. Checks include
degree sum=2E, agreement of gene degrees with full edges, the unique
7,399-gene domain, all 85 models and 83 donors, exact alpha weights, and
cross-input consistency of queries and categories.

For every contrast, gross gain minus gross loss equals new-minus-old
allocation. Each core and role partition independently sums to ALL;
the three rank-change strata sum to the complete category change.
Independent source state-level R totals provide a further reconciliation.
Synthetic cases cover degree ties, isolates, ranks, unequal model counts
per donor and opposing model-level flows.

Interpretation and outputs
The endpoints describe structural correspondence between connectivity and
allocation. Degree can change as part of the network update. The joint
comparison changes consumed evidence and graph state together, whereas
each conditional graph comparison holds the evidence state fixed. The
common binary graphs specify the DawnRank and degree calculations;
PRODIGY uses wider weighted backgrounds.

Outputs contain complete category/contrast records, input hashes and
independent numerical comparisons. This finite descriptive analysis
estimates neither causal mediation nor a population sampling distribution.
