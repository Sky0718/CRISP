Exact allele identity and functional-evidence coverage

This analysis links the complete candidate input pool to genomic allele
identities and the corresponding functional evidence in a fixed CIViC
snapshot. Matching eligibility uses the allele and evidence record,
independently of allocation gain or loss. The annotation analysis is
retrospective and post-exposure.

Input population
Allocation_and_Graph_Controls/Candidates/inputs/
accepted_qualifying_variant_records.csv contains 184 source records,
57 genes and 144 unique chromosome/position/ref/alt alleles. Identical allele
queries are deduplicated while every source record and model/gene link
remains represented. The pool includes candidate records and EGFR, KRAS and
PTPN11 context records; its denominator is this input pool rather than the
full universe of method selections. Genome assembly is identified from
each source VCF header, with unresolved assembly recorded explicitly.

Sources and snapshot
The source snapshot is the CIViC 01-Oct-2026 monthly release. Source tables
are VariantSummaries, MolecularProfileSummaries,
AcceptedClinicalEvidenceSummaries, AcceptedAssertionSummaries and the
accepted VCF where available. Source metadata include exact URL, retrieval
UTC, SHA-256 and any unavailable record. CIViC curated data use CC0 1.0.

ClinGen Allele Registry provides canonical identity links through anonymous
read-only HGVS queries:
https://reg.clinicalgenome.org/doc/AlleleRegistry_1.01.xx_api_v1.pdf
Requests contain public genomic HGVS expressions only, without model,
donor, sample or allocation context. Cached responses and errors retain
URL, status, time and hash.

Identity rules
Official GRCh38 assembly documentation supplies chromosome-to-RefSeq
accessions. Source assembly and exact sequence alleles determine genomic
HGVS for SNVs and supported simple variants. Ambiguous or unsupported
normalisation leaves an unresolved identity.

An eligible G identity is either an exact same-build normalised allele or
an exact canonical registry identity connecting source and CIViC
representatives. Returned sequence/build and local representation must
agree. Coordinate-only cross-build joins, gene/codon/exon-only records,
generic MUTATION records and complex profiles are ineligible as exact
allele matches. Protein names are metadata; protein-only matching is
outside this analysis.

All exact CIViC matches are retained when the source gene agrees with the
CIViC gene feature. An alias-based interpretation requires documented
identifier equivalence.

Functional evidence
An eligible functional assignment uses accepted evidence of type Functional
attached to a simple molecular profile containing one exactly matched
variant. Other accepted evidence types remain coverage metadata, including
Predictive, Diagnostic, Prognostic, Predisposing and Oncogenic records.

Evidence records retain ID, PMID/source, molecular-profile and variant IDs,
significance, direction, level, rating, statement, flags and review dates.
Supports GOF, LOF, Unaltered, Neomorphic and Dominant Negative are distinct.
Does Not Support GOF retains its original direction rather than being
converted to LOF. Unknown significance remains unknown. Broad categorical
or multi-variant AND/OR/NOT profiles have explicit exclusion reasons.

An exact match with an accepted Functional record establishes database
coverage. Assigning a functional direction to the study allele additionally
requires the cited primary experiment to support that direction in the
applicable context. Evidence from the original organoid study retains a
separate source flag.

Coverage, uncertainty and conflicts
Evidence interpretation distinguishes six states:
1. No canonical identity.
2. Canonical identity without an exact CIViC record.
3. Exact CIViC record without accepted Functional evidence.
4. Accepted Functional evidence pending primary-source/context verification.
5. Curated function with verified primary-source/context support.
6. Conflicting or context-dependent evidence.

Missing evidence remains unclassified. Sequence consequences and
gene-level cancer roles are separate annotations. Compatible functional
types and conflicting findings retain their assay contexts and source
flags. A flagged record requires resolution before an unqualified
functional interpretation.

Coverage is reported against 144 unique alleles, 184 source records and
57 genes, preserving duplicate records and gene/model multiplicity.
A single annotated allele cannot determine uniform activation for a
model/gene carrying several alleles. Cancer Hotspots and CGI annotations
are outside this source specification.

Consistency checks and interpretation
The source records, allele and gene totals, return mappings, genome build,
exact-match links, accepted status and simple-profile eligibility determine
the coverage counts. Automated summaries stop at database coverage and
retain unresolved identities and unavailable records. Primary-experiment
interpretation follows the evidence rules above.

Allocation comparisons apply the same annotation to both arms and retain
the original donor weights. They are descriptive post-exposure associations.
Input-pool identity coverage and verified functional-evidence coverage are
separate endpoints.
