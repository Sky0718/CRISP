Donor composition and continuous depletion analysis

The analysis partitions changes in pooled functional fractions into
within-donor changes and changes in donor representation. It uses fixed CRC
allocation ledgers, the donor index and 10,000 paired whole-donor draws.
Rankings, queries, categories, populations and seed averages are inputs;
missing outcomes remain separate from measured zeros. The comparison is
exploratory and was defined after exposure to the allocation results.

Role-level allocation
The old and joint-update functional_content/ledger.csv records define five
exclusive roles: ONCOGENE, TSG, DUAL_ONCOGENE_TSG, OTHER_OR_UNSPECIFIED and
UNLISTED. Exact rational sums give returned, positive, measured-zero and unknown
allocation for every available policy. Each role partition reconciles with the
complete population totals.

Donor decomposition
The three CRC policies are PRODIGY, strict DawnRank and full-query degree.
For each policy and each focal class BOTH_CORE and ONCOGENE, all 83 donors
are classified by old/new nonzero allocation support: common, entrant, exit
or neither. Let p_t=sum_d b_dt/sum_d r_dt, a_dt=r_dt/sum_d r_dt and
f_dt=b_dt/r_dt where r_dt>0. With pbar=(p0+p1)/2,

 delta p = sum_both [(a1+a0)/2 * (f1-f0)]
         + sum_both [(f1+f0)/2-pbar] * (a1-a0)
         + sum_enter a1*(f1-pbar) - sum_exit a0*(f0-pbar).

These four terms are within-common-donor fraction change, allocation
reweighting, entry and exit. This exact midpoint-centred decomposition is
descriptive. Zero-allocation fractions are undefined. The same identity
applies to continuous depletion -LFC with f_dt=s_dt/r_dt; complete continuous
measurement of both focal classes is a prerequisite checked from the sources.

Continuous donor-composition family
The 12 coordinates comprise three policies, two focal categories and two
new-minus-old endpoints: weighted -LFC sum and pooled -LFC mean. Models have
equal weight within donor and the 83 donors have equal weight. Every endpoint
uses the same 10,000 paired donor draws, with ratios recomputed in each draw.
Fixed original-donor scales are sample SD/sqrt(83). For a ratio difference,
the donor influence is

 (s1-Q1*r1)/R1 - (s0-Q0*r0)/R0.

The common multiplier q is the linearly interpolated 0.95 quantile of the
maximum absolute standardized deviation across all 12 coordinates.
Untruncated envelopes are estimate +/- q*scale. Original or resampled zero
denominators and degenerate scales are explicit undefined cases; donors,
draws and coordinates remain represented. This family measures sensitivity
to cohort composition, separately from other endpoint families.

Outputs and consistency conditions
CSV/JSON tables contain the complete role levels and donor-decomposition
terms. Resampled endpoint arrays retain the 10,000 draws and all 12 coordinates.
Exact source totals and allocation conservation constrain the summaries;
direct donor-index and multiplicity calculations provide two evaluations of
each continuous resample. Population identifiers, seed means and source
checksums accompany the numerical records.
